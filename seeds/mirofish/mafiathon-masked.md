The creator, event name, dates, and celebrity names are masked. The facts below were publicly available before
each event began. The final subscription outcomes are deliberately excluded.

## Challenge 1
- A top livestream creator announces the second edition of a month-long marathon.
- The stream will run continuously, 24 hours a day, for 30 days.
- It runs during a normal subscription month, without the platform's annual September subscription discounts.
- A cinematic announcement trailer features an internationally known reality-TV celebrity.
- The creator openly frames the event as an attempt to retake the platform subscription record after another
  streamer recently surpassed his earlier record.
- His first marathon previously peaked at roughly 306,000 active subscriptions.

## Challenge 2
- The same creator announces the third and advertised final edition of the month-long, 24/7 marathon.

  active subscriptions. This prior result was public before Challenge 2 began.
