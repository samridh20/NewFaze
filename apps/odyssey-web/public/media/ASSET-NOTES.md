# Preview asset provenance

The local JPEG posters are crops of the user-supplied nextFAZE concept mockups. They are illustrative concept artwork, not original FaZe footage or actual fan submissions.

Original shot and tutorial playback use the official YouTube links supplied in the project conversation. Actual live gallery playback comes from the existing campaign API. Replace concept artwork and provisional marks with approved brand/media assets before a public launch.

The Google Fonts Barlow Condensed and IBM Plex Mono files are distributed with their OFL license texts in `public/fonts/`.
